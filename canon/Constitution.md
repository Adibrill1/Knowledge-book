# Constitution

Status: Draft Canon  
Layer: I - Constitution  
Scope: Whole Brill ecosystem

## Purpose

The Constitution defines the enduring commitments of the Brill ecosystem.

It exists to protect identity, coherence, and trust across all future books, specifications, educational material, AI systems, standards, tools, and living documentation.

## Constitutional Commitment

The Brill ecosystem treats knowledge as a living architecture.

Knowledge is not merely accumulated. It is related, governed, interpreted, revised, and made usable across time.

## Core Commitments

1. Preserve meaning across change.
2. Separate stable canon from living commentary.
3. Make provenance visible.
4. Make relationships explicit.
5. Let artifacts evolve without losing identity.
6. Prefer clarity over volume.
7. Prefer durable structure over temporary convenience.
8. Treat implementation as part of the philosophy, not an afterthought.

## Canonical Separation

Canon defines what must remain coherent.

Commentary explores what may change, expand, and be contested.

No living interpretation should be mistaken for constitutional authority until it has passed through review, refinement, and deliberate canonization.

## Stewardship

The repository is stewarded as a knowledge commons with constitutional discipline.

Stewardship means protecting:

- conceptual integrity
- editorial quality
- navigability
- traceability
- historical continuity
- future extensibility

## Amendment Principle

Constitutional change should be rare, explicit, justified, and traceable.

An amendment must explain:

- what changed
- why it changed
- what prior understanding it replaces or refines
- which downstream artifacts may be affected

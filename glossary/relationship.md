## Short Definition

A relationship is an explicit connection between artifacts, concepts, claims, examples, or implementations.

## Expanded Explanation

Meaning emerges through relationships.

A document is easier to trust and use when it shows what it depends on, what it expands, what it illustrates, what it contradicts, what it implements, and what it supersedes.

Relationships prevent the repository from becoming a pile of isolated files.

## Canonical Relationship

The principle that relationships are first-class is defined in `../canon/Principles.md`.

Relationship mapping is one of the core practices that turns documents into knowledge objects.

## Usage Note

Relationships should be named when the connection affects interpretation.

Not every link is a meaningful relationship. A relationship should tell the reader why the connection matters.

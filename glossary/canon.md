## Short Definition

Canon is the stable, minimal body of material that defines the identity and coherence of the Brill ecosystem.

## Expanded Explanation

Canon is not everything that is important.

Canon is the material that must remain coherent across books, specifications, educational material, AI-facing systems, diagrams, patterns, cases, and the living digital edition.

Because canon carries constitutional authority, it should change slowly and deliberately. It should be concise enough to preserve identity without absorbing every explanation, example, or debate.

## Canonical Relationship

Canon is governed by:

- `../canon/Constitution.md`
- `../canon/Governance.md`

Canon is interpreted by commentary and implemented through patterns, workflows, templates, chapters, and the digital edition.

## Usage Note

Do not treat a polished document as canonical merely because it is well written.

Canonization is a governance decision, not an editorial mood.

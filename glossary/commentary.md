## Short Definition

Commentary is living interpretive material that explains, explores, applies, challenges, or extends canon.

## Expanded Explanation

Commentary gives the Brill ecosystem room to learn.

It may include research, examples, chapter notes, case studies, diagrams, implementation details, educational material, contested interpretations, and provisional language.

Commentary may evolve quickly, but it should remain traceable to the canon or question it is interpreting.

## Canonical Relationship

Commentary does not govern canon by default.

It can influence future canon through review, refinement, RFCs, and deliberate canonization.

## Usage Note

Commentary should not be treated as weaker than canon.

It has a different role: it protects the system's capacity to grow without forcing every living idea into constitutional authority.

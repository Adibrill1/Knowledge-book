## Short Definition

Provenance is the visible origin, lineage, and change history of an artifact or idea.

## Expanded Explanation

Provenance lets readers and systems understand where knowledge came from and how it entered the repository.

It may include a source manuscript, originating conversation, prior document, RFC, contributor, date, decision, revision, or migration note.

Provenance builds trust by making origin and evolution inspectable.

## Canonical Relationship

Provenance implements the constitutional commitment to make origin and change visible.

It supports governance by allowing future contributors to understand why an artifact exists and what it replaced, refined, or expanded.

## Related Terms

## Usage Note

A provenance note does not need to be long.

It should be specific enough that a future reader can understand the artifact's origin without reconstructing the entire conversation that produced it.

## Short Definition

Identity is the stable recognizability of an artifact or idea across change.

## Expanded Explanation

An artifact with identity can evolve without becoming unrecognizable.

Identity is supported by names, paths, metadata, relationships, status, version history, and provenance.

In Knowledge-book, identity matters because the same idea may appear as canon, chapter prose, glossary term, diagram, pattern, RFC, site element, or AI-readable context.

## Canonical Relationship

The principle that knowledge has identity is defined in `../canon/Principles.md`.

Document identity is implemented through editorial standards and reusable patterns.

## Usage Note

Changing a file name, title, status, or location can affect identity.

Substantive identity changes should be traceable.
